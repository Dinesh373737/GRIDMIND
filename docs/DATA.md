# Replay data and forecast preparation

GRIDMIND runs from local data. Downloads are a separate preparation operation and require internet access at setup only. The checked-in `data/austin_public.json` contains 120 replay intervals of 15 minutes for a virtual locality in the Austin area. Times are UTC in storage.

| Input | How the run uses it | Source |
|---|---|---|
| NOAA High-Resolution Rapid Refresh | Hourly forecasts at the Austin grid point. Cloud cover, hourly precipitation, temperature, 10 m wind/gusts, solar irradiance, and 80 m wind available to the wind curve. Each row retains model issue and valid times. | [Public NOAA archive](https://registry.opendata.aws/noaa-hrrr-pds/), [University of Utah Zarr guide](https://mesowest.utah.edu/html/hrrr/zarr_documentation/html/python_data_loading.html) |
| EIA-930 ERCOT | Actual demand in MW at the end of each reported hour. The packaged replay scales it to a virtual locality demand profile using a fixed reference from August 23. | [EIA historical data](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/930-content/about.html), [2018 July–December CSV](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/gridmonitor/sixMonthFiles/EIA930_BALANCE_2018_Jul_Dec.csv) |
| ORNL EAGLE-I | Quarter-hour counts for Travis County, FIPS 48453, used as a replay signal for the virtual feeder. August 24 has five consecutive reports of at least 300 customers without power between 20:00 UTC and 21:00 UTC, rising to 1,412 at 21:00 UTC. A below-threshold report at 21:15 UTC provides the recovery marker. | [Oak Ridge National Laboratory archive, version 4, CC BY 4.0](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.24237376.v4) |
| Pecan Street | Optional local overlay from minute-level Austin household panel and solar measurements. The simulator scales the measured aggregate to participants' registered capacities. Prepared output stays under `.runtime/` because the publisher's Kaggle listing leaves the redistribution license unspecified. | [Publisher's guide](https://www.pecanstreet.org/2025/07/public-data/), [Austin sample](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pecanstreetinc/ps-data-10-homes-1min-frequency-aug-2018) |
| µGIM pilot | Optional offline P2P benchmark from five office participants in Porto, June 3–9, 2019. The preparation script exports hourly observed demand and generation, forecasts, planned purchases and sales, and deduplicated transaction records. It keeps its original hourly clock. | [Gomes and Vale, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](https://zenodo.org/records/3371222) |

Run `python scripts/prepare_data.py --local-profiles` to prepare the last two sources under `.runtime/datasets/`. Each archive's SHA-256 is recorded. The runtime automatically uses the Austin meter overlay when it exists. The µGIM export is a separate matched-trade reference under that directory. It is kept separate from the Austin quarter-hour replay so it does not combine measurements from different times and countries.

The pilot import yields 835 complete, unambiguous participant-hours and 794 distinct logged fills. Four expected participant-hour keys are missing or incomplete and one has conflicting source rows. The preparation records the conflicting key and leaves those hours unfilled.

To regenerate the checked-in public pack, install the preparation-only readers with `python -m pip install numpy numcodecs pyproj eccodes`, then run `python scripts/prepare_data.py --refresh-public`. The preparation fetches the relevant range of the original county archive, individual Zarr weather chunks, and the original GRIB2 messages for irradiance. The simulation worker carries only compact numeric values.

The weather pack uses 00:00 and 12:00 UTC forecast cycles with a conservative one-hour publication delay. Hourly values are carried across the quarter-hour slots with their original target times. Solar estimates apply irradiance and a temperature derate. Wind estimates apply a virtual turbine's cut-in, rated speed, and cut-out. When the meter overlay is installed, a least-squares calibration estimates local PV output from matched August 23 values. A band based on the day's residuals gives a scenario range for the solar estimate. The sample is too short to establish a production prediction interval with tested coverage. Without the overlay the solar forecast is the physical curve estimate.

On the 29 available hourly forecast-target pairs in the replay, the calibrated PV estimate's normalized mean absolute error was 0.0589, compared with 0.1012 for carrying forward the latest normalized output. The scenario band covered 9 of 29 target readings. These are results for this short holdout window. Reliable probability statements for local solar or wind shortfall need a longer matched archive and interval calibration.

The optional weather stress scenario transforms successive 90-minute blocks into archived baseline, cloudy, rainy, and windy conditions. A run's seed fixes the intensity within each block. Cloud and rain reduce the archived irradiance and corresponding virtual PV yield. Windy blocks increase wind and gusts together and use the turbine curve for the virtual generation response. The outlook uses the regime at its target time. It retains its valid time and clears the NOAA issue time for any altered forecast. Event provenance preserves the original weather and power factors. The selected Austin window contains clear, sunny, and partly cloudy weather, so the rainy and windy blocks exercise simulated responses.

The grid risk calculation uses historical one-hour demand growth factors from a calibration window ending before the replay starts. Along with registered demand and the feeder capacity, these provide a local risk estimate under the simulation's assumptions. The county records report aggregate customers without power. They cannot identify the microgrid's feeder or the cause of a particular interruption. Missing county entries remain unknown. The chosen signal maps a real county-level interval into the virtual feeder's timeline.

Virtual participant loads, normalized generation, new participant schedules, and synthetic capacity or outage scenarios remain simulation records. The sources above are preserved separately. No measured outages or trades are produced by augmentation.
